Frequently asked questions

What markets does the app cover?

All 7 major pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, NZD/USD, USD/CAD), 15 minor crosses, 8 exotics (USD/ZAR, USD/MXN, USD/SEK, USD/NOK, USD/SGD and more), plus gold (XAU/USD) and silver (XAG/USD) — 32 instruments in total.

How are the buy/sell instructions generated?

Six weighted factors — trend, momentum, mean-reversion, breakout, ML probability and cross-sectional currency strength — are combined by market regime into a composite score. Beyond threshold, the system issues a full trade plan: entry, take-profit ladder, stop-loss, time-stop and flip-exits, with ATR-based position sizing.

How accurate are the predictions?

Direction models report honest walk-forward out-of-sample accuracy (typically 48–56% — the reality of FX forecasting). The edge comes from combining the model with structure, regime and risk: every plan carries a minimum 1.5 risk-reward, so you don't need to be right all the time.

Is this financial advice?

No. klevs FX trading App is an analysis and education tool. Everything it produces is a decision-support instruction, not personalized investment advice. Trading FX involves substantial risk of loss — never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes — monthly billing, cancel anytime from your account. Your access continues to the end of the current month.

How do I access the bot after subscribing?

Instantly. After checkout you get access credentials to the web app at /app — the live instruction feed, charts, backtest lab and risk dashboard work on desktop and mobile browsers. Elite plans also get API access.

Do you place trades for me?

No. The app tells you when to buy, what outcome to expect, and when to sell — execution stays with you and your broker. This keeps you in control and your capital in your own account.
