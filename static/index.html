    How this platform works

    1 · Trace — whole-market data engine

    Every instrument in the universe (7 majors, 15 crosses, 8 exotics, gold & silver) is loaded through a provider chain (Frankfurter/ECB → exchangerate.host → yfinance). When no live feed is reachable, a correlated currency-factor simulator generates a coherent entire market: each currency has a latent factor driven by shared risk & commodity shocks, and every pair is exp(L_base − L_quote) — so crosses are arbitrage-consistent and the strength meter has real structure to extract.

    2 · Predict — ML + statistical signals

    Per pair, a gradient-boosting ensemble (40+ features: multi-horizon returns, RSI, MACD geometry, Bollinger state, ADX regime, candle anatomy, session effects) forecasts direction, expected move and volatility over the next bars — trained strictly time-ordered (walk-forward, no shuffle) and reported with honest out-of-sample accuracy.

    3 · Act — signal engine & trade plans

    Six factors (trend, momentum, mean-reversion, breakout, ML, currency-strength differential) are weighted by regime into a composite score. Beyond threshold it emits a full plan: entry (next bar), stop-loss at 1.2–1.5×ATR beyond structure (wider when volatility is forecast hot), take-profit at 1.8–2.5×ATR with a guaranteed 1.5 minimum R:R, a partial-profit plan (50% at +1.5R → breakeven stop → trail to 3R), and ATR-based position sizing risking a fixed 0.75% of equity per trade.

    4 · Protect — portfolio risk layer

    Max open positions, per-currency exposure caps (no 3× USD stacking), correlation down-sizing, daily loss circuit-breaker and a drawdown throttle. The Kelly fraction is shown as a reference only (half-Kelly, capped).

    5 · Validate — event-driven backtester

    Signals computed on bar t execute at bar t+1's open (no lookahead). Intrabar stop/target fills are conservative (stop wins ties). Costs = instrument spread + slippage. Metrics: win rate, profit factor, expectancy in R, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown.
